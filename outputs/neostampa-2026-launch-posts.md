**Target audience:** Production managers, print shop owners, distributor partners

neoStampa 2026 is here.

Nine new or significantly updated capabilities — organized around the three things that determine whether a digital textile operation runs well: color accuracy, production throughput, and workflow control.

The features that matter most depend on what you print:

**If you run DTG or DTF:** There is now a dedicated DTG/DTF mode in the RIP. White-ink underbase management, pre-treatment consistency, and fluorescent ink support (yellow, magenta, orange, green) built into the workflow — not bolted on as an afterthought.

**If you run high-volume production:** Smart Reprint lets you scan a QR code to requeue a job with the exact original settings. No manual file retrieval, no settings mismatch. Remote queue management means you can approve, hold, or redirect jobs without being on the floor.

**If you manage color across multiple machines or substrates:** The new color matching system is designed for cross-machine consistency — one color standard across your fleet, across fabrics and materials, with fewer proofing rounds required to get there.

The new control center brings cost visibility and workflow management into a single interface.
