## Primary Segment This Release Matters Most To

**DTG/DTF & New Garment Decorators** — the dedicated DTG/DTF mode is a named feature, not just an improvement to existing functionality. This release also directly addresses the two most common acquisition blockers in this segment: white-ink reliability and color-drift problems. Combined with the fluorescent ink support (high-demand in activewear/streetwear), this release gives the DTG/DTF team more specific, demonstrable claims than any recent release. Effort should be weighted toward this segment in launch week.

**Second priority: Existing Customers** — the 5x PDF engine and remote queue management are operationally significant for any shop running volume. Smart reprint reduces costly reprint errors. These are credible upsell/retention arguments.

**Distributor Partners** are third — their content follows once the customer-facing messaging is locked, since distributor pitch language draws from both customer segments.

---

## Launch Sequence & Rationale

Existing customers are warmest — they already trust neoStampa and are most likely to act quickly on an upgrade prompt. They go first, 2-3 weeks before public launch, to create pre-launch momentum and surface any questions that can sharpen the public-facing messaging.

DTG/DTF segment goes at or around launch day — their channels (YouTube, peer forums, social) move on recency and are most effective when content is fresh. Public announcement and DTG/DTF content should be synchronized.

Distributor outreach runs concurrently with or just after launch week — they need the customer-facing materials in hand before they can carry the message to their own networks.
